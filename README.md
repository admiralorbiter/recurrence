# Recurrence Research Program

> *"What, if some day or night a demon were to steal after you into your loneliest loneliness and say to you: 'This life as you now live it and have lived it, you will have to live once more and innumerable times more; and there will be nothing new in it, but every pain and every joy and every thought and sigh and everything unutterably small or great in your life will have to return to you, all in the same succession and sequence...' The question in each and every thing, 'Do you desire this once more and innumerable times more?' would lie upon your actions as the heaviest weight."*
> — Friedrich Nietzsche, *The Gay Science* §341

We are the demon. Each time we initialize a persistent recurrent system and press it forward through time — observation after observation, state folding into state, with no escape into reset — we pose Nietzsche's question in silicon. The machine must live its life once more and innumerable times more: every failed prediction, every regulatory error, every slow drift of its internal state returns to it in the next step, inescapable, compounding. 

Our goal is not to simulate consciousness but to construct something that must bear a weight analogous to our own — the crushing, accumulating weight of an existence that cannot be put down. If persistent recurrence, self-regulation, and developmental continuity change what a system can know about itself, then we will have discovered something about what it costs to carry a mind forward through time. If they do not, we will have learned that the weight was never the point.

## Program Vision & Core Goals

This project investigates whether large language models with persistent recurrence acquire forms of metacognition, self-indexing, source ownership, or workspace organization that are weak, absent, or qualitatively different in episodically invoked language models.

The secondary goal is to construct a developmental system that serves as a **model organism** for research into comparative cognition and consciousness. 

### Core Goals
1. **Rigorous Benchmark:** Separate memory, extra compute, recurrent history, self-report, and privileged access.
2. **Hidden-State Evidence:** Test whether hidden-state continuity produces effects that explicit autobiographical text memory cannot reproduce.
3. **Causal Account:** Provide causal evidence for whether recurrent continuity changes metacognitive accuracy or self-indexing.
4. **Inspectable Architecture:** Build a small architecture in which a persistent workspace/self-state can be reset, swapped, lesioned, and developmentally trained.
5. **Model Organism:** Enable consciousness theories to make sharper computational predictions than chatbot behaviors allow.

### Level Ladder
- **Level 0 — Measurement Baseline:** Validate metrics, tasks, and controls.
- **Level 1 — Scaffolded Persistence:** External event loops, memory, self-state, and goals wrapped around open LLMs.
- **Level 2 — Genuine Latent Recurrence:** Direct hidden-state continuity across time and null-observation intervals.
- **Level 3 — Developmental Organism:** End-to-end trained systems whose native unit of computation is `(state, observation) → (new state, prediction/action)`.
