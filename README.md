# Recurrence Research Program

> *"What, if some day or night a demon were to steal after you into your loneliest loneliness and say to you: 'This life as you now live it and have lived it, you will have to live once more and innumerable times more...' The question in each and every thing, 'Do you desire this once more and innumerable times more?' would lie upon your actions as the heaviest weight."*
> — Friedrich Nietzsche, *The Gay Science* §341

This repository contains the complete specification, experimental design, and measurement plan for a research program investigating **persistent recurrent state, endogenous self-regulation, and developmental continuity** in artificial neural architectures.

The central question: **Does carrying a hidden state continuously across time create qualitatively different metacognitive monitoring, self-indexing, and source-ownership capacities than episodic prompt-level inference?**
