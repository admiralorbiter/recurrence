A second surprise

More information did not automatically help.

Everything at Once used about 730 prompt tokens—more than the Complete Diary's ~499—but scored only 66.7% overall versus the Diary's 81.0%.

Length, redundancy, ordering, and narrative conflict remain entangled here. H1 does not pretend this benchmark isolated which one caused the difference.
