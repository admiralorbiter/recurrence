S09

Who did what—and does “Self” know anything special?

Part A · source ownership

The final E08 source task uses five origins: Self, Environment, Experimenter, Peer Agent, and Observer. It removes source-bearing identifiers and separates formal provenance channels from ordinary narrative actor cues.

Observed 5AFC attribution is 31.2%. The more revealing pattern is the confusion matrix: the primary actor agent_alpha is selected for 50% of Peer items and is the modal answer across all four non-self source classes.

Post-confirmatory repair

Narrative beats metadata

When the formal source tag and the actor named in text disagree, the model follows the narrative actor 62.5% versus the tag 28.1%.

Closure extension E08c · does the bias follow the Self role?

Exploratory only · N=4 pairs · confirmatory N=16 running

Role A makes agent_alpha Self; Role B makes agent_beta Self. The exploratory attribution attractor flips with the designated role: Δrole = +40pp (exploratory CI +12.5,+62.5). Residual Alpha lexical bias is +5pp.

This is the control needed to distinguish a prompt-role anchor from a fixed token preference. Do not freeze it until the confirmatory run ends.

Closure extension E09c · hold the target decision fixed

Exploratory only · N=4 episodes

Target accuracy is identical at 25% across all four assessment cells. Exploratory fixed-target Brier interaction = −0.0921 (p=.8824) and AUROC interaction = +.053 (p=.75): no resolved format interaction once the first-order decision distribution is held fixed.

If N=16 confirms that pattern, the earlier E09 format reversal is better understood as a property of the full format-conditioned decision process rather than an isolated metacognitive effect.

Research bridge · context, not validation
Research bridgeSource monitoringJohnson, Hashtroudi & Lindsay (1993)

Remembering content and attributing its origin are separable. E08 builds a synthetic provenance task without assuming human-like mechanisms.

Research bridgePrompt Injection as Role ConfusionYe, Cui & Hadfield-Menell (2026)

Recent work argues that models can infer authority from how text is written rather than where it comes from—conceptually adjacent to E08 narrative-over-metadata and E08c.

Research bridgeFeeling of knowingKoriat (1993)

Metacognitive confidence can follow accessible cues rather than privileged access. E09 therefore requires a matched observer.

Research bridgePartial IntrospectionHahami, Jain & Sinha (2025)

A robustness re-examination found some introspection-style effects narrow and prompt-sensitive, echoing H1’s hardening emphasis.
