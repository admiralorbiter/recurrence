H1: Mnemosyne and the River

What survives when a language model is allowed to remember?

Full-H1 synthesis draft · August 17, 2026

S04–S09 canonical evidence is included. E08c and E09c appear as live closure extensions: exploratory results are shown, while N=16 confirmation is still pending.

You do not need a background in machine learning, statistics, cognitive science, or philosophy of mind to follow this site.

H1 begins with a practical idea: give a language model a past. Store events. Maintain goals. Keep a compact state. Let that state persist from one moment to the next.

Then do something less comfortable: keep trying to explain the impressive behavior in simpler ways.

We gave the machine a notebook, a diary, a dashboard, time to reflect, and a label called “Self.” Then we kept asking which of those things actually controlled what happened next.

The full H1 arc

01Memory

Explicit context helps. The raw transcript is a formidable baseline.

02Maintenance

A deterministic state machine persists cleanly; autonomous model-written state does not.

03Replay

Deterministic explicit continuity can be reconstructed from the event history.

04Reflection

Quiet explicit rewriting creates derived-state noise instead of reliable consolidation.

05Causal authority

History manipulations exert far more leverage than structured-state manipulations under balanced conflict.

06Ownership

Source attribution is weak, role-sensitive, and more influenced by narrative cues than formal metadata.

07Metacognition

Self-framing does not reveal a resolved positive advantage over a matched observer with the same public evidence.

The one-sentence result

Level-1 scaffolded persistence is useful, compact, inspectable, and causally readable—but the evidence so far does not make it an authoritative, private, irreducibly continuous internal state.

Why the unfinished experiments are already here

E08c asks whether S09’s “Self” attraction really follows the designated primary role rather than the token agent_alpha. Exploratory results say the attractor flips when the Self role flips. E09c holds the exact same first-order decision fixed across memory formats; its exploratory screen removes the earlier apparent format interaction. Both are visibly marked live rather than frozen.
