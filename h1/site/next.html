In plain English

H2 must show something visible prompt state cannot: hidden history dependence, selective causal leverage, or an observer-adjusted advantage based on private recurrent information.

The H2 Boundary

What latent recurrence now has to earn

Do not read this as a prediction.

H2 introduces a private hidden-state channel relative to a prompt-only observer. Whether that state becomes persistent, causally important, self-indexed, or introspectively accessible remains empirical.

H2.1 · Latent continuity

Can two systems with matched visible histories and current inputs behave differently because their recurrent hidden trajectories differ?

H2.2 · Causal recurrent leverage

Reset, clone, swap, interpolate, or lesion hidden state while holding visible history fixed. A strong result must be selective and on-manifold—not generic damage.

H2.3 · Privileged metacognition

Give the target access to recurrent state and a strong observer only matched public evidence. Then ask whether the target predicts its own future failures better. H0/H1 observer logic becomes the control battery.

What H1’s live closure studies decide

E08c determines whether the source-attribution attractor genuinely follows the designated Self role. E09c determines whether the earlier metacognitive format reversal survives when the exact same first-order decision is held fixed across formats.

A good negative H2 is allowed

If latent state is reconstructible from public history, interventions do not selectively steer behavior, source ownership remains cue-driven, and no observer-adjusted metacognitive advantage appears, the program should accept that boundary rather than escalate its vocabulary.
