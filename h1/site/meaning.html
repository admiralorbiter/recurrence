What Level-1 scaffolded persistence establishes—and what remains outside the claim ceiling.

Frozen evidencePost-confirmatory repaired analysisLive extension · confirmatory pending
In plain English

Level 1 is a strong engineering baseline, not a proof of selfhood. It gives H2 a more demanding set of tests.

What H1 Means

A self-state can be useful without becoming a private self

H1 intentionally pushes explicit prompt-level state as far as it can before native recurrence enters the program.

What Level 1 is good for

  • compact state serialization;
  • deterministic goal and memory maintenance;
  • causal interventions such as reset, clone, swap, and surgery;
  • source/provenance experiments;
  • observer-matched metacognitive benchmarks;
  • a clear baseline H2 must beat.

What did not emerge

  • an irreducible temporal dependency in deterministic state;
  • reliable autonomous self-maintenance;
  • useful quiet explicit consolidation;
  • state authority over a rich conflicting history;
  • clean five-source ownership;
  • a resolved positive self-framing metacognitive advantage under matched public evidence.

The claim ceiling

Under these Level-1 public-information protocols, the experiments do not provide behavioral evidence of a privileged private self-state.

That is deliberately narrower than “the model has no self,” “the model is not conscious,” or “all autoregressive models must behave this way.” Those claims are outside the instrument.

The deepest methodological lesson

An attractive cognitive story can be produced by a representation, prompt regularity, broken comparator, or statistical mistake. The project gets stronger when the claim shrinks to match the surviving mechanism.
