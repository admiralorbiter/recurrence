# 11. Theory Prediction Matrix

> This document establishes a pre-experimental mapping between empirical findings and major theoretical families in comparative cognitive science and consciousness research. To prevent post-hoc theory retrofitting, every major finding pattern is assigned explicit prior predictions and interpretations across theoretical frameworks.

---

## 1. Overview & Methodological Stance

Following the spirit of adversarial collaboration (e.g., Cogitate Consortium 2025), the project commits in advance to how different experimental outcomes alter credence across competing theories. 

A result does not "prove" a theory of consciousness. Instead, empirical outcomes raise or lower credence under specified auxiliary assumptions (Claim Level 7) or support functional/mechanistic analogies (Claim Level 6).

---

## 2. Theory Families & Indicators

| ID | Theory Family | Primary Computational Indicator / Requirement | Key Auxiliary Assumptions |
|---|---|---|---|
| **T1** | **Global Neuronal Workspace (GNWT)** | Global broadcast of selected internal representation to distributed subsystems; ignition dynamics; capacity-limited workspace bottleneck. | Information must be flexibly shared across downstream task heads rather than localized to a single head. |
| **T2** | **Higher-Order Thought (HOT)** | Higher-order meta-representation $M(S)$ that represents first-order state $S$ as occurring in the system; causal gating of first-order processing by higher-order state. | Meta-representation must be structurally or causally separable from first-order contents (double dissociation possible). |
| **T3** | **Recurrent Processing Theory (RPT)** | Sustained local or global recurrent dynamics (feedback loops) beyond feedforward pass; persistent state trajectory over time. | Recurrence must provide functional leverage (e.g. figure-ground, self-monitoring) not matchable by feedforward compute + memory. |
| **T4** | **Active Inference / Predictive Processing (AIP)** | Minimization of free energy / prediction error via hierarchical generative models; interoceptive regulation of internal variables. | Endogenous self-regulation variables (energy, integrity, goal tension) must shape precision weighting and policy selection. |
| **T5** | **Functionalist-Neutral / Control Theory (FNCT)** | Self-monitoring and executive control as purely functional engineering conveniences; no consciousness-relevant implications. | Metacognitive accuracy, source monitoring, and self-indexing emerge whenever task demand requires state tracking, without privileged access. |
| **T6** | **Skeptical Constraints (Campero et al. / Human Bridge)** | Objections stating that artificial neural representations lack genuine intentionality, phenomenal ground, or cross-substrate validity. | Behavioral/probe resemblances are artifactual or scaffold-driven; synthetic systems lack biological/developmental unity. |

## 3. Findings vs. Theory Matrix

| Empirical Finding Scenario | GNWT (T1) | HOT (T2) | RPT (T3) | AIP (T4) | FNCT (T5) | Skeptical (T6) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **F1: Workspace persists (J-space), but self-indexing (S-space) does not** | **Predicted:** Global broadcast does not require self-model. | **Tension:** Meta-representation should bind self-state. | **Compatible:** Recurrence can broadcast without self-tagging. | **Compatible:** Workspace handles task predictions without self-model. | **Predicted:** Workspace is functional routing; self-model unneeded. | **Predicted:** Disproves selfhood claims; shows standard LLM routing. |
| **F2: Double dissociation between higher-order monitor and first-order content (E22)** | **Compatible:** Workspace selection is distinct from content. | **Strongly Predicted:** Core prediction of HOT models. | **Compatible:** Separate recurrent loops for monitoring vs task. | **Compatible:** Hierarchical precision gating separated from state. | **Compatible:** Executive control head separated from task head. | **Tension:** Harder to dismiss as generic feature-probe artifact. |
| **F3: Recurrence effects fully reproduced by explicit text memory (H-A disconfirmed)** | **Tension:** Workspace requires dynamical ignition, not text buffers. | **Irrelevant:** HOT can operate over text-reconstructed memory. | **Falsifying for RPT:** Recurrence provides no unique leverage. | **Tension:** Interoceptive dynamics cannot be simulated by text. | **Predicted:** Memory equals continuity; latent state is redundant. | **Predicted:** Shows "persistence" was just prompt memory all along. |
| **F4: Metacognitive control succeeds without privileged access (Observer matches Self)** | **Compatible:** Broadcast reaches observer probes equally. | **Falsifying for Strong HOT:** Self-route must have privileged access. | **Compatible:** Recurrent state is fully reconstructible. | **Tension:** Interoceptive access should be internally privileged. | **Predicted:** Metacognition is public calibration, not private introspection. | **Predicted:** Confirms LLMs have no private internal self-access. |
| **F5: Privileged access & self-indexing emerge only under direct supervision (Installation vs Emergence)** | **Compatible:** Workspace routing can be trained. | **Compatible:** Meta-representations can be supervised. | **Tension:** Natural dynamics should induce recurrence self-indexing. | **Tension:** Generative self-model should emerge from active inference. | **Predicted:** Capabilities are installed features, not emergent selfhood. | **Predicted:** Demonstrates synthetic "self-models" are engineered artifacts. |
| **F6: Endogenous quiet-time updates ($z_{t+1}=R(z_t, \varnothing)$) reorganize goals & reduce future error** | **Compatible:** Offline workspace consolidation. | **Compatible:** Offline meta-state updating. | **Strongly Predicted:** Recurrent feedback processes unresolved state. | **Strongly Predicted:** Free-energy minimization during null observations. | **Compatible:** Background state maintenance algorithm. | **Tension:** Non-trivial latent update during silence is hard to dismiss as prompt-following. |

## 4. Methodological Commitments

1. **No Retrofitting:** If an experiment produces Outcome F3 (memory matches recurrence), RPT credence must be lowered in the final report; the project will not redefine "recurrence" to include text buffers.
2. **Theory-Specific Credence:** Result reports must explicitly state: *"This result increases credence in [Theory X] under the assumption that [Indicator Y] is valid, but is neutral under [Theory Z]."*
