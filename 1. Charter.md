## 1. Purpose

The project studies whether **persistent recurrent state, endogenous self-regulation, and developmental continuity** change the computational capacities that humans often associate with conscious cognition—especially metacognitive monitoring, higher-order representation, source ownership, and a stable self-model.

The program uses artificial systems as **model organisms for comparative cognition**. It does not assume that current models are conscious, that the proposed systems will become conscious, or that any one functional property is sufficient for consciousness.

The central methodological opportunity is that artificial systems can be cloned, reset, state-swapped, lesioned, replayed, and trained under controlled developmental histories in ways that are impossible in human research.

## 2. North-star question

> **Does a persistent recurrent developmental trajectory causally produce more general, privileged, or genuinely higher-order representations of a system's own cognitive states?**

Supporting questions:

- Can hidden-state history matter after explicit memory and current input are held constant?
- Does recurrent continuity improve metacognitive accuracy, or merely task performance?
- Can a system distinguish its own internal state from an externally matched anomaly?
- Can a monitor state be separated causally from the first-order state it monitors?
- Does self-indexing emerge developmentally, or only when directly trained?
- Do endogenous variables create useful ongoing dynamics, or merely artificial reward pressure?
- Which findings generalize across transformer, recurrent, state-space, and recurrent-depth architectures?

## 3. What the project is trying to build

The long-term engineering target is a **Persistent Developmental Cognitive System**. The more provocative working term is a **synthetic developmental organism**.

The term *organism* is earned operationally, not rhetorically. A candidate system should eventually satisfy most of the following:

1. **Persistent bounded state:** an internal state continues from one update to the next and is not fully reconstructible from a transcript.
2. **Endogenous dynamics:** the state can change during intervals without meaningful external input.
3. **Self-maintenance variables:** the system regulates internal variables whose violation affects its future viability or competence.
4. **Environment coupling:** it receives observations, takes actions, and experiences delayed consequences.
5. **Individual history:** two initially identical copies can develop systematically different policies or self-models through different histories.
6. **Developmental plasticity:** its internal representations or policy change over its lifetime, not only during centralized pretraining.
7. **Self/world boundary:** it can track which variables, actions, memories, and effects are attributable to itself versus the environment or other agents.
8. **Counterfactual inspectability:** its state can be reset, copied, swapped, or lesioned to test causal claims.
9. **No dependence on continuous verbalization:** the system can maintain and transform state without turning every intermediate step into text.
10. **Claim discipline:** none of the above is treated as proof of phenomenal experience.

The first versions will not meet all ten criteria. The research goal is to discover which criteria matter for which cognitive capacities.

## 4. Fixed epistemic commitments

These rules should be harder to change than any individual hypothesis.

### 4.1 Separate evidence levels

For every result, label the strongest supported level using the canonical **Claim Ladder** defined in [2. Definitions.md](file:///c:/Users/admir/Github/recurrence/2.%20Definitions.md#L319-L373):

- **Level 0 — Interface effect** (prompt/scaffold changes response)
- **Level 1 — Behavioral capacity** (prespecified task above baseline)
- **Level 2 — Internal encoding** (probe recovers state information)
- **Level 3 — Causal mechanism** (state intervention alters target behavior)
- **Level 4 — Privileged self-related mechanism** (internal route unavailable to observer)
- **Level 5 — Higher-order / self-indexed organization** (monitor/self state causally gates first-order state)
- **Level 6 — Cross-system mechanistic analogy** (converging behavioral & causal analogy)
- **Level 7 — Theory-specific consciousness relevance** (raises/lowers credence under specific theory)
- **Level 8 — Phenomenal inference** (supports claim about experience)

A result at Level 3 does not automatically support Level 5, 7, or 8.

### 4.2 Prefer dissociation over resemblance

A human-like answer is weak evidence. A double dissociation—property A without B, and B without A—is much more informative.

### 4.3 Hold external evidence constant

Claims of privileged access require conditions in which the target system and a matched observer have the same visible evidence, while only the target has the relevant internal state.

### 4.4 Prefer forced choice and continuous measures

Avoid binary yes/no self-report when steering, prompt style, or character can bias affirmation. Prefer localization, ranking, comparison, calibration, and prediction of independently known latent variables.

### 4.5 Treat language as one output channel

Natural-language explanation is useful data, but it is not the ground truth of the mechanism. Use logits, probes, intervention effects, exact-choice tasks, and hidden-state measures alongside prose.

### 4.6 Make negative results first-class

A failed effect narrows the architecture or construct. Negative results remain in the permanent record with the same metadata as positive results.

### 4.7 Separate exploration from confirmation

Exploratory work can search prompts, layers, strengths, and metrics. Confirmatory work freezes those choices before evaluating held-out items, seeds, and model variants.

### 4.8 State the system boundary

Every result must say whether it concerns the base model, model plus context, model plus memory, scaffolded agent, recurrent state, online learner, or full environment-coupled system.

## 5. Program-level hypotheses

The program begins with several hypotheses, not beliefs.

### H-A: Memory is not continuity

A persistent hidden state can carry causal history that is not reducible to explicit autobiographical memory.

### H-B: Recurrence is not merely extra compute

When compute and visible evidence are matched, the temporal organization of latent state can change path dependence, source attribution, or self-monitoring.

### H-C: Workspace and self-model are separable

A globally available representational space can exist without a stable self-indexed monitor; continuity and post-training may alter the coupling between them.

### H-D: Metacognitive access is graded and domain-specific

Models may have privileged access to some internal variables while relying on reconstruction or shared task cues for others.

### H-E: Development matters

Some self-model properties may be attractors reached through a persistent history rather than static capabilities installed entirely by training labels.

### H-F: Endogenous regulation shapes selfhood

Internal variables that must be regulated may provide a stable computational reason to distinguish self from world, but may also create brittle reward-seeking or artificial confounds.

## 6. Success criteria

Project success is not contingent on reaching Level 3.

### Minimum successful outcome

- a reproducible benchmark that separates explicit memory, scheduled computation, recurrent state, and self-report;
- at least one clean negative or positive result that survives transcript-matched controls;
- a public methodological account of what the result does and does not establish.

### Strong successful outcome

- a causal demonstration that persistent latent history changes a metacognitive or self-indexing capacity beyond memory and extra compute;
- replication in at least two architectures or matched base/post-trained variants;
- state reset/swap evidence that identifies the relevant mechanism.

### Transformative successful outcome

- a small developmentally trained system in which persistent state, endogenous regulation, and self-modeling can be independently lesioned;
- a result that forces at least one consciousness theory or metacognition theory to sharpen a computational prediction.
