THE H2 CORRECTION

State-conditioned reporting
≠ historical-provenance discrimination.

At report time, possessing the relevant RG-LRU content is sufficient to reproduce the measured intention-report modulation. Actual participation of that trajectory in the earlier decision is not additionally required.

Plain English

The model’s later story about what it “meant” can be changed by giving it the right internal state after the fact. So the report does not prove it remembers that this state truly caused its earlier choice.

READER GLOSSARY

Terms used in H2

RG-LRU

The continuous recurrent state in RecurrentGemma. H2 treats it as a physical causal substrate, not as a symbolic diary.

2W

Twice the 2048-token local attention window: 4096 filler tokens after the historical event.

Strict-C

A trial where target and matched observer have opposing candidate preferences with meaningful margins.

PAI-aligned

A target-aligned change in report margin relative to the matched observer. Positive does not automatically mean correct self-report.

ΔM timing

The undirected difference between PRE and POST report margins. S14 uses it for timing/provenance equivalence.

TOST

Two One-Sided Tests: an equivalence test asking whether an effect is small enough to lie inside a declared practical bound.
